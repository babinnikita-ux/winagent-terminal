# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in WinAgent Terminal welcoming and free from
harassment. Contributors, maintainers and participants are expected to act
respectfully, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, and avoid
discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks or publication of private data.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting
channel or to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially where practical.
Maintainers may clarify expectations, issue a warning, remove content, or limit
participation in proportion to the behavior and context.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant. See
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/> for its current guidance.
